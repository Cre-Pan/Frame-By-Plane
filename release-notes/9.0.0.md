# 🎞️ Frame By Plane — 9.0.0

Frame By Plane 9.0.0 brings **plane groups** you can animate as one piece, an exact **Layer Transform** panel, **Set Layer Origin**, smooth **Infinite Rotation** and keyframed **Opacity** on image layers that finally plays back. Top and Side views stay fast with many layers, and errors that used to stay in the console now show in Project Doctor. It installs on **Blender 5.2 LTS** and **Blender 5.3**. Everything from 8.5.1 is listed in the [8.5.1 release notes](8.5.1.md).

## 🆕 New in 9.0.0

- 📦 **Plane groups.** Ctrl+G on some layers makes a group with a **box rig**: the outline of its nearest and farthest plane joined at the corners. A click on a plane selects the whole group, a **double click enters it** to work on single planes while the others fade (also in Solid shading), with a **GROUP** badge styled like Solo. Esc goes up one level.
- 🎛️ **Group Controller.** One button (Layer List side bar, or the sidebar's Item tab) adds an Empty that the group's layers follow without moving. Move, rotate, scale and **keyframe** it to animate the whole group; its **Group Opacity** fades every layer of the group and can be keyframed too. A click on the group selects the controller, so G, R, S and I act on the group.
- ✂️ **Ungroup:** Ctrl+Shift+G on a selected group dissolves it; every layer stays where it is.
- 👁️ **Solo a group:** Z Pie Solo with a group selected shows the whole group.
- 📐 **Layer Transform** in Properties > Object and in the sidebar's Item tab: exact Location, Rotation and Scale of the layer, keyable with I, shown in the Graph Editor and Dope Sheet. Alt or Copy All to Selected sets every selected layer.
- 🎬 **Advanced Render** (Output Properties): off by default, so Blender's own Output settings are used; turn it on to build folder and file name. Prefix, Name and Suffix are stacked with a checkbox each, Prefix and Suffix get a Letter / Number / Both token ("Shot A1"), and the Separator menu shows examples. Frame Range, File Extension and Background Render left the panel.
- 🔔 **Sound when the render finishes:** an optional short chord tells you a render ended well.
- 🎯 **Set Layer Origin** (right click > Frame By Plane > Layer): Origin to Geometry, Origin to 3D Cursor and Geometry to Origin, without moving the drawing. The Crop effect has an **Origin to Centre** button.
- 🔗 **Link Timelines:** a link button at the right of every timeline header (Timeline, Dope Sheet, Graph Editor, NLA, Sequencer), on by default, keeps them aligned frame by frame: the playhead and Start/End line up even when one editor has a wider channel list or an open sidebar. Scroll, zoom or resize any of them and the others follow at once, without lagging behind. If the scene is used as a clip in the Sequencer of another scene, the Timeline lines up under that clip.
- 📏 **Grease Pencil Quick Shape.** Draw a line, an arc (also of an ellipse), an ellipse, a rectangle or a zig-zag polyline and keep the pen still at its end: in the first 10 seconds of the stroke it becomes a clean shape that follows the cursor until you release, with only its own points (a line has 2, a rectangle 4, an ellipse 24, an arc 3 Bézier). The **mouse wheel** changes the number of points, **Shift** moves it slowly for precision, **Ctrl** snaps angles and makes circles and squares, and the **Quick Shape** panel (Adjust Last Operation) changes Points and Curve Type after release, and switches between Line and Arc when the stroke could be either.

## 🛠️ Better and fixed in 9.0.0

- 🎚️ **Keyframed Opacity on image layers plays back and renders.** Before, the layer kept the last value of the slider at every frame. Solid shading follows it too.
- 🔄 **Infinite Rotation is smooth** during playback and keeps the drawing's proportions on a layer stretched on one axis.
- 🏷️ **Top and Side views with many layers:** names are laid out up to ten times faster and never end up on top of each other, even with dozens of layers close together. Moving the mouse no longer recomputes them.
- ⚡ **Faster with many layers:** importing several sequences in a row no longer keeps Blender busy afterwards, playback spends about half as much time in the add-on and scrubbing about a quarter less, clicking through layers with an Alpha Outline box no longer rebuilds it each time and a new outline takes less time to build, long sequences import twice as fast, image effects cost less during playback and are quicker to add, and Blender starts faster.
- 🔲 **Rig styles:** a Legacy layer shows only its coloured wire; an Alpha Outline box sits around the drawing, not the whole canvas.
- 🎭 **Shape masks work like a rig.** Square, Circle and Triangle masks resize from their corner and side dots and move when you drag inside them, without box select; G on layer and mask together no longer breaks the shape. Adding a mask keeps you on the layer's Modifiers, and removing the mask's modifier row removes its helper too.
- ✏️ **Grease Pencil Mask works like a mask:** draw a shape and the layer shows only inside it (Invert hides it instead). It is in Add Modifier > Mask, next to Clipping Mask. Leaving Draw Mode in any way returns to the layer and shows the masked result; its row is called "GP Mask" and has a single Invert.
- 🔘 **Mask dots stay the same size** on screen at any zoom, and **Edit Grease Pencil Mask** is on top of the mask panel.
- 📍 **Lattice handles match the other rigs:** Grid points, corners and Puppet Pins are filled dots of constant size on screen, like the plane and mask rigs, and can be dragged in every view.
- 🧹 **Cleaner mask panels and outlines:** one clean Circle and Triangle outline, no repeated hints, no node-group picker on effect rows.
- 🗑️ **Deleted planes leave the Layer List**, even when pasted, appended or deleted outside Frame By Plane; the delete button on a stale row removes it.
- 🩺 **Project Doctor** is one panel at the bottom of the Output tab, closed until you need it, with a single Scan button, a clear summary and filters. It no longer flags layers that do not cover the whole Scene range.
- 🩺 **Project Doctor > Runtime messages** lists the errors caught while drawing the viewport or changing frame in this session, with how many times they happened.
